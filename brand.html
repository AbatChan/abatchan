Everything needed to represent abatchan correctly: the name, the statement, the symbol, the lockups, the colour system, and the type scale. Files are linked where they are described.

The lowercase treatment is deliberate. It carries calm confidence and premium simplicity, and it stays consistent everywhere the brand appears.

03 · the symbol

Mirrored, open geometry.

Built from the letters in “aba”, the symbol reads as connection, modular systems, and the relationship between frontend and backend. It is balanced, and it can expand.

Clear space around the symbol is at least one inner curve width on every side. The motion above is the standard reveal; it finishes on indigo and holds.

05 · colour

Two carry it, one signals.

Black and soft white do most of the work. Indigo is a signal, not a background theme, and no other accent is introduced unless a product interface needs semantic states.

Primary Black
#0D0D0D
Dark backgrounds, UI foundations, premium surfaces, video backgrounds.
Soft White
#F5F5F3
Primary type on dark backgrounds, light surfaces, logo contrast.
Accent Indigo
#6366F1
Active states, key interaction points, signal dots, links, restrained glow.
Gradients are not used as generic decoration. On light backgrounds indigo is darkened to #4F46E5 for body-size text so it stays readable.

07 · usage

The short version.

If a use is not covered here, keep the mark legible, keep the space around it, and leave the geometry alone.

Do

  • Use the white mark on #0D0D0D backgrounds.
  • Use the black mark on #F5F5F3 or white backgrounds.
  • Keep clear space of at least one inner curve width on all sides.
  • Use indigo as an accent or a final motion state.
  • Reach for the symbol alone at small sizes: avatars, favicons, app icons.

Don’t

  • Stretch, rotate, or otherwise distort the mark.
  • Recolour it outside the three brand colours.
  • Add drop shadows, outlines, or effects.
  • Place it on busy media without enough contrast.
  • Rebuild the wordmark by typing it · use the supplied lockup.
