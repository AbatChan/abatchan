your licence

The sites your licence is running on.

Enter your licence key to see which websites are using it, and to release one when a site moves or a client finishes. Your key is checked each time and is never stored here.

    Releasing a site frees its activation immediately. Nika keeps running on that site: it simply stops counting against your licence, and stops receiving updates until the key is entered there again. Development, staging and local installs never use an activation, so they will not appear here.
