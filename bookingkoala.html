When independent BookingKoala help makes sense

You may already have the platform but not a dependable customer journey. The booking section may need to match the website, the quote form may collect the wrong details, an integration may be incomplete, or a previous setup may simply need repair.

I work on the implementation around that real business flow: what a customer sees, what information is captured, where it goes, and whether the finished path works from beginning to end.

What can be scoped

  • initial BookingKoala setup and customer booking flow
  • booking sections and quote forms
  • website cleanup around an embedded or connected booking experience
  • agreed third-party integrations
  • repairs, refinements, and final verification

The final scope depends on the current setup, the required workflow, available access, and any other systems involved. If the surrounding website also needs work, that is identified and priced clearly instead of being hidden inside the BookingKoala task.

How the work runs

First I review the existing setup and the outcome you need. Then I define the exact flow, deliverables, access requirements, assumptions, and exclusions. Work is built against that scope and verified across the agreed customer journey before handover.

For a useful first message, include your current website, what is already configured in BookingKoala, the part that is not working, and the result you want customers or staff to get.

Useful details before we start.

Can you fix an existing BookingKoala setup?

Yes. Existing work is reviewed first, then the repair is scoped around the broken or unfinished parts and the customer journey that needs to work.

Can you connect BookingKoala to my existing website?

Yes, where the required website access and platform options are available. The quote will separate BookingKoala configuration from any wider website work so the scope stays clear.

Do you work only with cleaning businesses?

No. The service is for businesses whose BookingKoala workflow fits their operation, including cleaning and other service businesses.

What should I send for a quote?

Send your website, the current BookingKoala setup, what is not working or still missing, the required outcome, and any deadline. Screenshots or a short screen recording are useful when the issue is visual or flow-specific.
