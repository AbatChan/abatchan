pricing questions

Useful details before we start.

Why are the prices shown as starting points?

Two projects can look similar but have very different content, integrations, account systems, technical debt, or deadline pressure. The starting price gives you the correct budget range before the detailed scope is prepared.

How do payments work?

Payments are handled by invoice, not a generic checkout button. Medium and large projects are split into milestones tied to clear deliverables, and each invoice lists the available payment method.

Can I start smaller and add more later?

Yes, provided the first version is designed with the later phases in mind. The scope can be arranged as a focused first release followed by measured improvements.

Do you work with existing websites and products?

Yes. Existing work is reviewed first because poor structure, outdated plugins, missing documentation, or unstable integrations can affect the quote.

Tell me what needs to work.

You do not need a perfect brief. Share the problem, the current setup, and what success should look like.
