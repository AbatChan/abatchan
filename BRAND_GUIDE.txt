ABATCHAN BRAND GUIDE
Version 1.0


01. BRAND NAME

Primary display name: abatchan
Legal company name: ABATCHAN LTD

The brand is styled as one lowercase word: abatchan.
This is intentional. The lowercase treatment communicates calm confidence,
modern systems, minimalism, and premium simplicity.

Do not style the public-facing brand as "Abatchan", "ABATCHAN", or
"Abat Chan" unless a legal or formal document requires the registered name.


02. BRAND STATEMENT

abatchan is a modern engineering brand focused on building connected digital
systems through scalable infrastructure, intelligent design, and clean
execution.

Core line:
If it plugs in, I build it.

What abatchan builds:
- connected web and mobile products
- automation and workflow systems
- APIs and integrations
- operational dashboards and platforms
- scalable cloud and backend infrastructure
- developer tools, plugins, and digital products

abatchan is not only a website brand. It represents the engineering of
connected systems.


03. POSITIONING

Category:
Modern digital engineering, infrastructure, automation, and connected product
development.

Positioning idea:
Build connected systems.

Supporting messages:
- Modern systems. Clean execution.
- Infrastructure for modern workflows.
- Engineered to connect. Built to scale.
- From interface to infrastructure.
- Systems that work together.


04. BRAND PERSONALITY

The brand should feel:
- modern
- engineered
- minimal
- intelligent
- calm
- premium
- scalable
- technically confident

Avoid:
- crypto or Web3 aesthetics
- gamer visuals
- neon overload
- noisy glitches
- generic AI imagery
- aggressive or hyper-futuristic type
- corporate jargon and empty buzzwords


05. BRAND VOICE

Write with clarity and restraint. Be direct, specific, and solution-oriented.
The voice should sound like a capable engineer explaining what matters.

Good:
"We design the interface, build the system, and connect the workflow."
"A clean product layer backed by infrastructure that can scale."
"Build connected systems."

Avoid:
"We are revolutionising the future with cutting-edge, next-generation AI."
"Your ultimate digital transformation partner."


06. VISUAL DIRECTION

Visual territory:
- premium minimalism
- developer infrastructure
- dark UI systems
- monochrome-first compositions
- precise grids and structure
- cinematic but inspectable imagery
- subtle indigo signal accents

Use space deliberately. The work should feel organized, not empty.
Use diagrams, product interfaces, workflow architecture, code-adjacent details,
and real system states instead of decorative technology imagery.


07. COLOUR SYSTEM

Primary Black
Hex: #0D0D0D
Use for: dark backgrounds, UI foundations, premium surfaces, video backgrounds.

Soft White
Hex: #F5F5F3
Use for: primary type on dark backgrounds, light surfaces, logo contrast.

Accent Indigo
Hex: #6366F1
Use for: active states, key interaction points, signal dots, links, small
highlights, restrained glow, and logo motion finish.

Colour rules:
- Black and soft white carry most of the brand.
- Indigo is a signal, not a background theme.
- Do not add extra accent colours unless a product interface requires semantic
  states such as success, warning, or error.
- Avoid gradients as generic decoration.


08. TYPOGRAPHY

Primary typeface: Geist
Use for: wordmark support, product UI, website body text, navigation, labels,
technical information, and most digital applications.

Recommended weights:
- Geist Regular: body copy
- Geist Medium: navigation, labels, the abatchan wordmark, small headings
- Geist Semibold: key headings and calls to action

Secondary typeface: Satoshi
Use for: presentation headings, editorial moments, campaign art direction, and
supporting marketing typography.

Typography rules:
- prefer lowercase for brand-facing display language
- use clean hierarchy and normal tracking
- align intentionally; avoid decorative effects
- do not use condensed or aggressive display fonts


09. LOGO SYSTEM

The logo symbol is built from mirrored, open geometry inspired by "aba".
It represents connection, modular systems, APIs, workflows, flexible
infrastructure, and the relationship between frontend and backend.

The symbol communicates:
- balance and symmetry
- connected flow
- adaptable infrastructure
- a system that can expand

Required versions:
- primary lockup: symbol plus lowercase wordmark
- symbol only: avatar, favicon, app icon, watermark
- white version: dark backgrounds
- black version: light backgrounds
- outline version: premium, technical, or blueprint-like uses
- transparent version: overlays and mockups

Logo rules:
- keep clear space around the symbol equal to at least one inner curve width
- use the white mark on #0D0D0D backgrounds
- use the black mark on #F5F5F3 or white backgrounds
- use indigo as an accent or final motion state, not everywhere
- never stretch, rotate, recolour randomly, add drop shadows, or put the logo
  on visually busy media without enough contrast


10. LOGO MOTION

Primary logo animation: approximately 5 seconds at 30 fps.

Timeline:
0.00-2.00 seconds
The symbol draws in soft white in this sequence:
1. bottom curve
2. left curve
3. center bridge
4. right curve

2.00-2.15 seconds
The completed symbol shifts left to create room for the wordmark.

2.28-4.15 seconds
A restrained white-to-indigo signal moves through the symbol in the same
bottom-to-left-to-center-to-right sequence. Each component settles to #6366F1.

3.17-4.24 seconds
The wordmark "abatchan" in Geist Medium eases in from the right with opacity.
It is not typed character by character.

4.24-5.00 seconds
Hold the completed lockup: indigo symbol, soft-white wordmark, black field.

Motion principles:
- engineered, smooth, and controlled
- use a single signal pulse, not a chaotic flicker
- use gentle easing with a very small overshoot only where it feels natural
- no excessive glow, particles, distortion, or glitch effects


11. DIGITAL / WEBSITE DIRECTION

The site should feel like a sophisticated engineering studio and product team.

Recommended layout traits:
- dark and light modes, with black as the primary cinematic backdrop
- large but disciplined editorial type
- system diagrams and real interface previews
- subtle indigo indicators for interaction and status
- clean grid systems
- one clear action per section

Core website language:
Headline: Build connected systems.
Supporting copy: We design and engineer the interfaces, infrastructure, and
integrations behind modern digital products.
Primary CTA: Start a project
Secondary CTA: View systems


12. SOCIAL CONTENT DIRECTION

Social presence should be premium, system-oriented, and dark-mode native.

Strong content categories:
- logo and brand system details
- interface and dashboard previews
- workflow / architecture diagrams
- project breakdowns: problem, system, result
- automation and integration concepts
- development process clips
- short technical opinions about building scalable products
- before-and-after product transformations

Instagram / Behance visual formats:
- black or soft-white fields
- precise grid overlays used sparingly
- indigo signal dots and active states
- close-up logo construction frames
- type specimens using Geist and Satoshi
- diagrams that explain a real workflow

Do not post generic motivational graphics, random code screenshots, or content
that does not support the system-building point of view.


13. CONTENT EXAMPLES

Post headline:

Post headline:
"From workflow to infrastructure."

Post caption:
"abatchan builds the connected layer: product interfaces, APIs, automations,
and the infrastructure that keeps them working together."

Notification-style campaign copy:
Title: Build connected systems.
Body: Interfaces, infrastructure, and integrations engineered to work as one.


14. FUTURE EXTENSIONS

The identity can extend without changing its core:
- abatchan labs
- abatchan systems
- abatchan studio
- abatchan infra

Keep "abatchan" as the recognisable core in all extensions.


15. QUICK BRAND CHECKLIST

Before publishing anything, check:
- Is it lowercase "abatchan" for public brand use?
- Does it use #0D0D0D, #F5F5F3, and #6366F1 correctly?
- Is Geist used for digital work, with Satoshi reserved for support/editorial work?
- Does it look engineered and calm rather than flashy?
- Does every visual say something about a connected system, product, workflow,
  or infrastructure?
- Is indigo used as a signal rather than the whole design?
- Is the message direct and technically credible?

Final statement:
abatchan is a modern engineering brand focused on building connected digital
systems through scalable infrastructure, intelligent design, and clean
execution.
